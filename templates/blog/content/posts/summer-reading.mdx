Short notes, in the order I read them.

## Fiction

- **The Lighthouse Keeper's Year.** Slow, quiet, worth it for the last chapter.
- **Small Rooms.** Linked stories about one apartment building. Read it in a day.

## Non-fiction

- **The Shape of Attention.** One good idea stretched to a book; read chapters 1 and 7.
- **Working in Public Libraries.** The best book about software I read this year, and it isn't about software.
