This site is a folder of text files and one config file. It builds in under a
second and costs nothing to host.

## What a small site needs

1. A way to write that you'll actually use.
2. A URL that won't change.
3. A feed, so readers can follow without an account.

Everything else is optional.

## Keep the URL

Pick your domain and your URL scheme once. A post at `/notes-on-small-websites`
should still be there in ten years. Redirects are a promise you'll forget.

## Skip the analytics

I used to check visitor counts daily. Now I read replies instead. The number
never changed what I wrote; the replies sometimes do.

export default defineConfig({
