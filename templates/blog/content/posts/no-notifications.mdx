I turned off every notification on my phone and laptop for seven days.
Calls from three people still rang.

## Days one and two

I checked my phone more, not less. Without a badge to tell me nothing had
happened, I had to look.

## Day four

The checking stopped. Email went from forty glances a day to three sessions:
morning, after lunch, end of day.

## What came back on

| App | Back on? | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Calendar | Yes | Meetings start whether I look or not |
| Messages from family | Yes | They rarely write; when they do it matters |
| Email | No | Three sessions a day is plenty |
| News | No | It was never urgent |

## The rule I kept

A notification has to be about a person waiting for me, or a time that
won't move. Everything else can wait for me to look.
